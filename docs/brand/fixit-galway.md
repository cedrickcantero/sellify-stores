# FixIt Galway brand guideline (demo store)

FixIt Galway is a made-up phone repair and resale shop on Shop Street, Galway.
This guideline is for its public online store only. It is deliberately
different from the Sellify platform guideline (`docs/brand/sellify.md`): the
Sellify backend is purple, neutral and businesslike; FixIt Galway is teal,
sand and coral, warm and local.

Everything below is applied through the Sellify store editor (Online Store
page). Nothing in the store is hand-coded, so each rule names the editor
setting that carries it.

## Personality

A friendly local repair counter by the Atlantic. Honest prices, quick fixes,
a person who knows your name. Calm and practical, never salesy.

## Logo

`docs/brand/fixit-galway-logo.svg`: a teal tile with two waves (sand and
coral), then the wordmark "FixIt" in teal and "Galway" in deep sea ink, set in
Roboto Slab, with a small caps strapline.

- Upload it in **Store details, Logo**. It sits on the sand background.
- Keep the tile and the wordmark together, and keep clear space around the
  logo at least as wide as one wave.
- Do not recolour it, stretch it, put it on coral, or add a shadow.

## Colours

| Role | Name | Code | Editor setting | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Atlantic teal | `#0F766E` | Brand, Primary colour | Buttons, links, selected choices, the logo tile |
| Accent | Claddagh coral | `#F97360` | Brand, Accent colour | The banner and small highlights only |
| Background | Salthill sand | `#F5EFE6` | Brand, Background colour | Page background |
| Text | Deep sea ink | `#1C2B2A` | Brand, Text colour | Body text and headings |

Contrast (WCAG):

- Deep sea ink on sand: 12.9:1. Teal on sand: 4.8:1, so teal works for links
  and button fills. White on teal buttons: 5.5:1 (the store picks white
  automatically).
- Coral is a fill, never text. Coral on sand is only 2.4:1. The store puts
  near-black text on the coral banner automatically (6.9:1).

## Type

- Headings: **Roboto Slab** (editor: Brand, Font pair "Slab"). Sturdy and
  friendly, like a hand-painted shop sign.
- Body: Inter (the store's body font for every pair). Plain and easy to read
  on a phone.
- Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps headings; the only caps are the
  logo strapline.

## Shape

- Corner radius **12** (editor: Brand, Corner radius). Soft, not bubbly.
- No heavy shadows, gradients or patterns. Sand and white space do the work.

## Tone of voice

Talk like the person behind the counter: short, warm, plain. Say what
happens next.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Cracked screen? We'll have it back to you the same day." | "Industry-leading screen restoration solutions." |
| "10% off screen repairs this week" | "MEGA SALE!!! LIMITED TIME ONLY" |
| "Drop in to Shop Street and we'll pay you on the spot." | "Submit your device for valuation processing." |
| "Book a repair" | "Click here to initiate a booking" |
| "Sorry, that time was just taken. Pick another?" | "Error: slot unavailable." |

## Store content (as entered in the editor)

- **Store name:** FixIt Galway
- **Banner (on):** "10% off screen repairs this week"
- **About:** "We're a small repair shop on Shop Street, fixing phones for
  Galway since 2015. Most screens and batteries are done the same day. We
  also sell refurbished phones with a 12-month warranty, and we'll buy your
  old one."
- **Contact:** Shop Street, Galway. A phone number and an email address.
- **Opening hours:** Monday to Saturday 09:30 to 18:00, Sunday closed.
- **Tabs:** Shop, Repair and Sell all on.

## Photos

Real, bright product shots on a plain light background. One phone per photo,
front first. No stock imagery of people, no dark or busy backgrounds.
